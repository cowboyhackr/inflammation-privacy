Inflammation as a Conversation

Privacy policy

Effective and last updated: September 10, 2026

This policy explains how John McKenna (the developer, “we”) handles information in the Inflammation as a Conversation iPhone app.

Explore without an account, advertising, analytics or tracking. The educational collection works offline. If your version includes feedback, sending a report is optional and shares only the submitted information and accompanying report details described below.

Using the app

Illustrations, molecular structures and educational explanations are bundled with the app. We do not collect your browsing history within the collection. The app does not access HealthKit, medical records, contacts, photos, microphone, camera or location, and it does not use advertising identifiers or persistent device identifiers.

Version 1.0 has no in-app feedback submission feature. The feedback sections below apply to versions that include it, starting with version 1.1. You can use the full educational collection without sending feedback or providing an email address.

Information you choose to send

When you tap Send in a feedback, bug report, feature request or correction form, the app sends:

  • Your message and selected feedback category.
  • A supporting citation or reference and reply email address, if you enter them.
  • The app name, version and build, the entry screen, and a randomly generated identifier for that report. This identifier belongs to the report, not your device.
  • For a cytokine correction, the selected cytokine and section, displayed educational text, scientific source link and Protein Data Bank identifier, where applicable.

You can review attached details before sending. Unsent drafts are held in memory and are not saved between app launches. Submitted feedback is stored through the Consider service, whose backend assigns a record identifier and creation time. If you contact us by email, we also receive your email address and the contents of your message.

How information is used and shared

We use submitted information to review scientific corrections, investigate problems, consider feature requests, improve the app and respond when you provide a reply address. We do not sell personal information or use feedback for advertising or cross-app tracking.

Feedback travels over HTTPS to the Consider backend hosted on Render. Service providers involved in delivering, hosting and storing feedback process information to operate those services. Network requests necessarily expose connection information, such as an IP address, to the receiving infrastructure, and providers may maintain operational or security logs. See Render’s privacy policy.

Retention and your choices

We retain submitted feedback and support correspondence while needed to address reports, maintain the accuracy of the educational content and improve the app, unless a longer period is required by law. There is no fixed automatic deletion schedule.

You can stop future submissions by not using the feedback feature. To request access to, correction of or deletion of information you previously sent, email the privacy contact below. Include enough detail to locate the report, such as the reply address used, approximate submission date or report identifier. Because feedback does not require an account, we may need additional information to locate an anonymous report and verify that it is yours. Deleting the app does not delete reports already submitted.

External links and this website

Scientific references open external websites, including the RCSB Protein Data Bank and Reactome. Those websites handle information under their own privacy policies. Apple may process App Store, device or diagnostic information according to your settings and Apple’s privacy policy.

This policy page is hosted by GitHub Pages. We have added no analytics, advertising, cookies or tracking scripts to it. GitHub may process technical information when you visit, as explained in the GitHub privacy statement.

Children and health information

The app is an educational resource and does not provide diagnosis or treatment advice. Please do not include medical records or other sensitive health information in feedback. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has submitted personal information, contact us to request its removal.

Policy updates

We will update this page when the app’s privacy practices change and revise the date above.

Contact

For privacy questions or requests, contact John McKenna at Feedback@solalumina.com. Please include “Inflammation privacy” in the subject.
