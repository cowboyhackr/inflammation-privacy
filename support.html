Inflammation as a Conversation

App support

Explore the immune system through fourteen illustrated cytokine characters and their matching molecular profiles.

Contact support

Email Feedback@solalumina.com for help, bug reports, scientific corrections or feature requests. Please include “Inflammation support” in the subject.

For a problem with the app, include your iPhone model, iOS version, app version and the steps that led to the issue. Please avoid sending personal medical information.

Getting started

Choose Characters or Signals on the landing screen. Select a character to meet it, or select a molecular structure to rotate it and explore Receiving cells, Inside the cell and Related signals. Each character links to its matching signal, and each signal links back to its character.

Using the app offline

The core collection works without an internet connection. Links to scientific references open external websites and require internet access. In versions that include in-app feedback, sending feedback also requires internet access.

Privacy and educational use

Read our privacy policy for details about information handling and privacy requests. This app is for education and does not provide diagnosis or treatment advice.
